A few real rules from the [[checklists|checklists]], shortened. Next to the number: #repeat means the note came up twice, #script means the [[audit|audit]] checked the item.

- `sheets-menus#1` A menu from a button takes the button’s place: the menu’s top right corner matches the button’s top right corner. It does not drop below the button. #repeat #script
- `navigation#3` Every visible control shows what a tap will do. A button with nothing to show is unnecessary. #repeat #script
- `sheets-menus#2` Sheet headers have no text Cancel or Done: round symbols, ✕ on the left, ✓ on the right with an accent fill. #script
- `layout#5` A single glyph button in a toolbar is round, 44 × 44. A pill is only for a group of two. #script
- `layout#6` Small graphics are strictly 1 : 1: sliders, dots, radios, rings. #script
- `states#4` An empty state sits in the centre of the free area, not pressed to the top. #script
- `visual#4` One accent per screen. Colour marks the main action only.
- `texts#13` A term of our own is explained in the interface where it first appears.
