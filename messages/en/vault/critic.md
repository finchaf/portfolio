A separate run of the tool with a fresh context. It did not build the frames and did not know what was intended: it judged what it saw. It could not change the file, only measure.

## What it read

The [[checklists|checklists]] for its frame type, the task’s style and screen map, and the neighbouring frames of the branch.

## How it looked

Each frame large and alone, neighbours side by side in groups of 3–5. Grid and spacing, copy, what matters most and whether it reads in a second, consistency with neighbours. On onboarding, the paywall and the main screen, also dullness: does the screen have a hero, or is it a flat list of grey rows?

## How it answered

“Clean”, or a list of actionable edits, most important first: breaks the scenario, breaks product scope, geometry, visual, taste. No praise. An example from the made-up task:

```
07 · Night detail
  — button label 13 pt, needs at least 16       texts#5
  — Share hugs the right edge, margins unequal  layout#1
08 · Home — clean
```
