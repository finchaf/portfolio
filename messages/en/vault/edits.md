command: `edits`
when: After stage 1, at the stop, after stage 2
---

The job of edits was to apply everything, lose nothing, and not hear the same note on the next task.

1. **Sort.** What arrived became a numbered list: what exactly, on which frame. An ambiguous item went out as one question with a default, the rest was applied at once.
2. **Apply.** Structure changed the screen map, style changed the tokens, and the variables updated everywhere. Afterwards: a screenshot of every affected frame and an [[audit|audit]].
3. **A rule in the base.** For every note. It was written as a checkable statement, not a retelling, and went into the right [[checklists|checklist]] with its source.
4. **Summary.** What was applied, what was not and why, which rules were added.

> [!info] The second time
> If a note came up twice, the rule was not being read in time. The item got a repeat mark and moved up into the main rules or into an audit script.
