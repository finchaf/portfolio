# Code of conduct

Issues and Discussions here are for the site and how it is built. Be kind and specific, assume good
intent, and keep to the subject. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination of any kind are not
accepted, and neither is posting anyone's private information.

The owner moderates: a comment that breaks this may be edited, hidden or removed, and an account that
keeps breaking it may be blocked. To report something privately, write to
[work@finchaf.com](mailto:work@finchaf.com).
